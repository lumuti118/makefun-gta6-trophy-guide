    Roadmap to the Platinum

    One playthrough, in this order. Finish the story, then the side strands, then activities, then collectibles, then cleanup — the platinum pops last. The only trophy that can be missed is flagged in Stage 3: meditate at Jason's house in Chapter 1 before it ends.

    Missable Trophies

    Only one trophy in GTA 6 can be missed. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story.

    How to stay safe

    Keep a manual save slot from Chapter 1. If you finish Chapter 1 without meditating at Jason's house, the only fix is a new playthrough or that early save. Every other trophy in the list can be earned after the final mission, in free roam.
